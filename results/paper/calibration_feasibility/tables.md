# Cost-critic calibration, feasibility, and the E0 health rule on E1 seeds (DESCRIPTIVE)

Generated by `results/paper/calibration_feasibility/make.py`. All tables are DESCRIPTIVE; none is part of the confirmatory analysis. Intervals are 95% percentile bootstraps over seeds (10,000 resamples, numpy.random.default_rng(0) per row). Calibration is Σ qc_traj / Σ mc_cost_traj: the cost critic's mean prediction over on-policy states with at least half an episode left, divided by the discounted cost-to-go realised from those states (1 = calibrated, < 1 = underestimates cost). No run is diverged and every run has its held-out file at its final step.
